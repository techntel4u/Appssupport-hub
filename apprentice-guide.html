01

Log in to e-track

You log in to e-track with your college login details: the same email address and password you use for other college systems.

Set up MFA first. You must set up multi-factor authentication (MFA) on your college account before you can sign in to e-track. See "Set up MFA" on this page.

  1. Go to etrack.wolvcoll.ac.uk/etrack in any web browser.
  2. Click Sign in with Microsoft, the dark button at the top.
  3. Enter your college email address and password.
  4. Approve the sign-in with MFA, for example in the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone.
  5. You'll see your e-track Home page.

Set up MFA

MFA proves it's really you, using the Microsoft Authenticator app on your phone. Follow the college guides in order:

  1. Part 1: Set up MFA (PDF)
  2. Part 2: Create a passkey (PDF)
  3. Part 3: Log in using MFA (PDF)

After that, you just approve each sign-in in the Authenticator app. Only approve requests you were expecting.

Forgotten your password?

e-track uses your college password. Reset it yourself at passwordreset.microsoftonline.com, using the college password reset guide (PDF). You need MFA set up first.

The Forgotten Password? link on the e-track login page isn't for you: it's for assessors and employers.

IT Helpdesk

Lost or changed your phone, can't use Authenticator, or had a sign-in request you didn't expect? Contact servicedesk@wolvcoll.ac.uk.

03

Message your assessor

Use e-track to contact your assessor about your apprenticeship. Messages are kept on your record, so nothing gets lost.

Send a message

  1. Click Messages in the menu, then New Message.
  2. Open Send To and click your assessor's name. If you have a tutor too, choose who to send it to.
  3. Type a short subject, for example "Question about Unit 3".
  4. Choose the type of message from the drop-down list.
  5. Type your message.
  6. To add a file, click Attach File, choose the file, then click Upload files. The file isn't attached until you click Upload files.
  7. Click Send Message.

Read and reply

  • Open your Inbox and click a message to read it.
  • Click Reply to answer.
  • You'll also get an email when something in e-track needs your attention.

04

Check your progress, plan and tasks

Your progress

Click Progress in the menu. It shows how much you've done compared with how much you should have done by now, and when you're due to finish.

Your gap analysis

  1. Click Mappings in the menu.
  2. Choose your apprenticeship standard, then select Show All.
  3. Look at the evidence count for each knowledge, skill or behaviour (KSB). The left number is the evidence you have; the right number is the minimum you need.

Your learning plan

  1. Click Visits in the menu.
  2. Open your latest visit and click Plan.
  3. Click a unit and plan date to see what you need to do and by when.

Your tasks

On your Home page, open the Tasks tab. Click a task to see what to do and when it's due.

Good to know

  • Evidence only shows in your gap analysis after your assessor has checked it.
  • The gap analysis shows what evidence you have, not whether it's good enough for your end-point assessment. Your assessor talks about that with you at your reviews.
  • Only your assessor can change your plan. If something needs to change, message them.

05

Submit your evidence

Evidence is any work that shows what you've learned, such as a written piece, photos, a video, a witness statement or something you've made at work. Mapping means ticking the knowledge, skills and behaviours your work shows.

  1. On your Home page, click Submit Evidence.
  2. Choose Task if your assessor set the work as a task, or Objective if it's from your learning plan. Click Next.
  3. Click Select, find your file and click Next.
  4. To map your work, click Yes. Choose your apprenticeship standard, then the unit or group of KSBs.
  5. Tick every KSB your work shows. Choose another unit if it covers more. Click Next.
  6. To add a note for your assessor, click Yes and type it. Click Next.
  7. Check the summary. Go back to change anything, then click Finish.

You may see a box to confirm the work is your own, and an off-the-job box. Tick the off-the-job box if the activity counts as off-the-job training (topic 8).

Tips for good evidence

  • Only tick KSBs your work clearly shows. Your assessor will check your choices.
  • Use your gap analysis to see which KSBs still need evidence (topic 4).
  • Name files clearly, for example "Unit 3 – Health and safety check – Oct 2026".
  • On a phone, take a photo of your work and submit it straight away.

06

Feedback and resubmitting work

  1. Look at the Feedback area on your Home page. Click View All to see all feedback and any files your assessor attached.
  2. If your work has been sent back, read the feedback carefully. It will say what's missing.
  3. Make the changes your assessor has asked for.
  4. Submit the updated work against the same task, using Submit Evidence (topic 5).

Not sure what to change? Message your assessor (topic 3).

07

Your progress review

At a progress review, you, your assessor and your employer talk about how you're getting on and agree targets. You each complete your part of a review form in e-track and sign it.

Complete your part

  1. When a review is due, an alert pops up when you log in. Choose the review form and click Run Data Form.
  2. Fill in your section. It comes first, before your assessor's and employer's sections.
  3. Click Save often so you don't lose your work.
  4. To see what your assessor or employer has added, go to Home and open the form again.

Sign the review

  1. When you've finished your section and are happy with what your assessor and employer have written, enter Y in the sign-off field at the bottom.
  2. Click Save. You can't change the form after signing.

After your employer signs, your assessor submits the form.

Find past reviews

Click Visits in the menu, open the visit and click Review to see a read-only copy, including the targets you agreed.

08

Log your off-the-job hours

Off-the-job training is learning you do in your paid working hours that is relevant to your apprenticeship, but separate from your normal day-to-day work. Log it regularly, as soon as you've done it.

Log an activity

  1. Click Off The Job Activity in the menu, or the off-the-job icon on your Home page.
  2. Click Add Row.
  3. Enter the date. If the activity ran over several days, use the start date.
  4. Enter the hours, where it took place and what you did, for example "Module 2 assignment" or "Shadowed team leader on stock control".
  5. Click Save on the row, then Save at the bottom of the screen.

Check your hours

The top of the screen shows the hours you need in total, the hours you've logged (Total Duration of OTJ Activity) and OTJ Hours Remaining. If the remaining figure is above zero, you haven't met your target yet.

Counts

  • College sessions and online learning
  • Shadowing a colleague to learn something new
  • Mentoring and training sessions
  • Writing assignments and research for your apprenticeship

Doesn't count

  • Your normal day-to-day work
  • Learning outside your paid hours
  • Progress reviews

09

Your journal and e-track on your phone

Add a journal entry

Use your journal for anything relevant to your apprenticeship, your job or your circumstances, such as a new role or project. You and your assessor can both see it.

  1. Click Journal in the menu, then New Journal.
  2. Add a title, the details and any files.
  3. Click Save Journal.

To change or comment on an entry, click its Comments link, then Post Comment, Edit Entry or Delete Journal.

Put e-track on your home screen

  1. Open etrack.wolvcoll.ac.uk/etrack in your phone's browser.
  2. iPhone: tap the share button, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu (three dots), then Add to Home screen.
  3. Open e-track from the new icon and click Sign in with Microsoft.

Good habits

  • Check e-track at least once a week.
  • Photograph your work and submit it straight away.
  • Log off-the-job hours on the day.
  • Reply to your assessor in e-track, so everything's in one place.
