What is e-track?

e-track is the online portfolio that holds everything about an apprentice's programme in one place.

It is part of Tribal's Maytas system, so you'll also see it called Maytas Hub. Assessors, apprentices and employers all log in to the same record, each with their own view.

It's where apprentices submit evidence, where progress reviews are completed and signed, where off-the-job training is logged, and where assessors track how every apprentice on their caseload is doing.

Log in to e-track

e-track runs in any web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. There's nothing to install.

Which login do I use?

The login page has two ways in. Which one you use depends on who you are.

Apprentices

Use the Username and Password boxes under "Or enter your details below", with the details allocated to you. Don't use Sign in with Microsoft. How to log in

On a phone? Open the address in your browser and add it to your home screen, so e-track opens in one tap like an app.

Who uses e-track, and for what

Everyone sees the same apprentice record, but each person does different things with it.

Assessors

Manage their caseload, plan and record progress reviews, check and map evidence, set RAG ratings, message apprentices and monitor off-the-job hours.

Apprentices

Submit and map evidence, check their progress and plan, complete and sign progress reviews, log off-the-job training and message their assessor.

Employers

Follow their apprentice's progress, add their comments to progress reviews and sign them off.

What e-track does

Six things you'll use most.

Tracks progress

Shows actual progress against expected progress, and the date an apprentice is projected to finish.

Holds the evidence

Apprentices upload work and map it to the knowledge, skills and behaviours (KSBs) of their standard.

Records reviews

Progress reviews are planned as visits, completed by all three parties and signed digitally.

Logs off-the-job hours

Apprentices log training as they go, and e-track works out the hours still to do.

Flags risk

RAG ratings and risk levels show at a glance which apprentices need support first.

Keeps messages together

Messages between assessors and apprentices stay on the record, with an audit trail.

Words you'll see in e-track

A quick reference for the terms used in e-track and in these guides.

KSBs
Knowledge, skills and behaviours: what the apprenticeship standard says an apprentice must show.
Mapping
Ticking the KSBs or criteria a piece of evidence shows. Apprentices suggest, assessors confirm.
Gap analysis
The Mappings screen: evidence counts against each KSB, showing what still needs evidence.
Progress review
A regular meeting between apprentice, assessor and employer, recorded as a visit with a signed review form.
RAG rating
Red, amber or green: the primary assessor's overall judgement of how an apprentice is doing.
OTJ
Off-the-job training: learning in paid working hours that is relevant to the apprenticeship, logged in e-track.
Caseload
The apprentices assigned to you, listed on Browse Learners.
Gateway and EPA
Gateway is the point an apprentice is ready for end-point assessment (EPA), the final independent assessment.
